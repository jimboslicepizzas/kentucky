Explore Pike County

Things to Do in Pikeville, Kentucky

From breathtaking mountain gorges and engineering marvels to legendary history and trails.

Nature & Views

Breaks Interstate Park

Known as the "Grand Canyon of the South." Features stunning overlooks, deep gorges, hiking trails, paddle boating, and incredible mountain vistas.

Landmark

Pikeville Cut-Through Overlook

A massive engineering marvel that reshaped Pikeville. Visit the overlook for breathtaking panoramic views of the city below—especially stunning at sunset.

Adventure

Hatfield-McCoy & Hillbilly Trails

Rugged Appalachian ATV/UTV trail systems minutes from your cabin door. Perfect for off-road enthusiasts looking for mud, thrills, and mountain views.

More Local Favorites in Pikeville

Dueling Barrels Brewery Craft spirits, tastings, and restaurant in downtown Pikeville.
Bob Amos Park Hiking, mountain biking, and picnic overlooks right in town.
Big Sandy Heritage Museum World's most complete Hatfield & McCoy feud artifact collection.
Appalachian Center for the Arts Live concerts, theater, and regional art exhibitions.
