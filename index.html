Container Dining & Entertainment

Nothing beats a hot meal and a cold drink after a thrilling day on the four-wheelers. Hang out at our vibrant, open-air shipping container food court featuring string lights, communal fire pits, live music, and incredible food for the whole family.

Jimbo Slice Pizza & Sandwiches

Our flagship vendor serves authentic 12" Neapolitan-style pizzas crafted with imported San Marzano tomatoes, as well as hearty, handcrafted sandwiches—perfect for fueling up before hitting the trails.
