Local Attractions

From adrenaline-pumping off-road trails to serene Appalachian lakes, your adventure starts here.

⛰️

Hillbilly Trails

Hundreds of miles of interconnected, multi-use off-road trails winding through the Appalachian mountains. Perfect for our UTV rentals.

 🛶

Fishtrap Lake State Park

Just a short ride away, experience pristine fishing, boating, and kayaking at Fishtrap Lake and the surrounding lush wildlife areas.

Experience the heart of Eastern Kentucky. Check out local artisan shops, historic sites, and vibrant community events right on Main Street.
